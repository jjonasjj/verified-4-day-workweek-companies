Most remote job aggregators scrape expired listings, route applicants through spammy email walls, and mix "compressed 4x10 (40-hour)" schedules with true **32-hour reduced-hour 4-day workweeks at 100% full-time salary**.

1. **True 4-Day Week (`32h @ 100% Pay`)** — Friday-off or flexible 4-day schedules without salary prorating.
2. **100% Async (`Zero Daily Standups`)** — Written-first engineering, design, and operations teams.
3. **No Degree Required** — Skills-first hiring with transparent salary bands.
4. **Entry-Level & Junior Remote** — Genuine career-starter remote positions.
5. **Night Shift / Flexible Timezone Remote** — Roles for nocturnal and global async workers.
